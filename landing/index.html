Why it feels different

Built for speed, privacy and flow.

Runs in your browser

Parsing and rendering happen client-side with Web Workers and canvas. No server round-trips, no waiting on uploads.

Private by design

Your files stay on your device. Anonymous sessions live only in your browser and clear when you close the app.

Multi-document tabs

Keep a tree, a matrix and a genome open at once. Switch instantly between every open document from one shared tab bar.

Survives a refresh

Your open workspace is restored after an accidental reload, with sensible limits so storage never bloats.

Search, filter, export

Find motifs in sequences, filter variants and matrix rows, and export FASTA, GFF3, TSV or PNG straight from each viewer.

Consistent & learnable

Every viewer follows the same toolbar · canvas · side-panel layout, so the second tool feels familiar the moment you open it.

Quick start

From file to figure in three steps.

Pick a tool

Open the app and choose Sequence, Tree, Matrix or Sanger from the home screen — or drag a file straight in.

Open your file

Select a local file. AriaDNA detects the format, parses it in the background and opens it in a new tab.

Explore & export

Zoom, search, recolor and annotate. When it looks right, export a figure or a cleaned-up data file.

● No account needed
