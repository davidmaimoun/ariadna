The toolkit

One workspace, multiple viewers.

Open as many files as you like in parallel tabs — a tree here, a matrix there, a genome alongside. Every viewer shares the same layout: a toolbar, the canvas, and a collapsible side panel.

Sequence

FASTA & GenBank viewer

Pan and zoom across whole genomes, read individual bases, see annotations, GC content, ORFs and live translation. Multi-FASTA contigs switch with a click.

.fasta · .fa · .fna · .gb · .gbk
Alignment

Multiple sequence alignment

Colored columns, consensus, and per-column conservation at a glance. Scroll thousands of columns smoothly; zoom from overview to single residue.

.afa · .aln · .msa · aligned .fasta
Phylogenetics

Tree builder & viewer

Compute Neighbor-Joining, UPGMA, MST (Kruskal/Prim) and GoeBURST from a distance matrix — or load a Newick tree. Rectangular, circular and force layouts, with annotation overlays.

.nwk · .tre · .tsv · .csv
Matrix

Distance & allelic heatmaps

Render distance or allelic-profile matrices as a heatmap, a sortable table, or presence/absence. Recolor, reorder, search rows, and export a publication-ready PNG.

.tsv · .csv · .tab
Sanger

Chromatogram viewer

Open AB1 traces, inspect the four-channel chromatogram, read quality scores, and edit base calls. Export the corrected read as FASTA.

.ab1
Variants · Reads · BLAST

VCF, SAM/BAM & BLAST

Triage variants with type and quality filters, inspect read pileups and coverage, and browse BLAST tabular / XML / JSON hits sorted by identity and e-value.

.vcf · .sam · .tsv · .xml · .json
Why it feels different

Built for speed, privacy and flow.

Runs in your browser

Parsing and rendering happen client-side with Web Workers and canvas. No server round-trips, no waiting on uploads.

Private by design

Your files stay on your device. Anonymous sessions live only in your browser and clear when you close the app.

Multi-document tabs

Keep a tree, a matrix and a genome open at once. Switch instantly between every open document from one shared tab bar.

Survives a refresh

Your open workspace is restored after an accidental reload, with sensible limits so storage never bloats.

Search, filter, export

Find motifs in sequences, filter variants and matrix rows, and export FASTA, GFF3, TSV or PNG straight from each viewer.

Consistent & learnable

Every viewer follows the same toolbar · canvas · side-panel layout, so the second tool feels familiar the moment you open it.

Quick start

From file to figure in three steps.

Pick a tool

Open the app and choose Sequence, Tree, Matrix or Sanger from the home screen — or drag a file straight in.

Open your file

Select a local file. AriaDNA detects the format, parses it in the background and opens it in a new tab.

Explore & export

Zoom, search, recolor and annotate. When it looks right, export a figure or a cleaned-up data file.

● No account needed

Open your first genome now.

AriaDNA launches instantly in your browser. Bring a FASTA, a Newick tree or a distance matrix and start exploring.
